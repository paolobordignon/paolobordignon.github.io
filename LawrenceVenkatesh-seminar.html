Seminar on Faltings-Lawrence-Venkatesh methods

The following page contains the information for a running seminar in Fall 2025 in Leiden on Faltings-Lawrence-Venkatesh machinery [LV20].
The seminar is organised by Paolo Bordignon, Alexander Molyakov, Felix Kalker and Victor de Vries.
