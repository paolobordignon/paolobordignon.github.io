A connecting thread

Changing the frame.

The connection to arithmetic geometry may be a habit of attention: a concrete object reveals different relationships when its representation changes. A sample recontextualized by Madlib, a reflection in a Leiter photograph, a world built by Borges—each invites a change of perspective. This is an analogy between ways of looking, rather than a claim that all these works share one meaning.
